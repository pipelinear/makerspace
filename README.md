A student club for design, fabrication, electronics, and art. Explore the tools in our room, the projects we’re planning, and a Robotics course in the making.

**Golden Time** · Tuesdays & Fridays, during homeroom. Beginners welcome.

The final two pages are for member signatures and images. Click **Upload**, choose a PNG, JPG, or WebP, and send. A successful upload becomes lined paper, folds into a 3D paper plane, and flies out of the book. The small help note credits [ShaderLabs](https://shaderlabs.app/) to Ewan McCorkell, Makerspace student.

The [signing desk service](signature-service/README.md) connects shared uploads and saved layouts through Cloudflare while the site stays on GitHub Pages. Follow the [setup instructions](signature-service/SETUP.md) to connect it; apply all four database migrations when updating the service. With no service URL, sending reports that uploads are unavailable; it does not show a successful flight.

The admin entrance is the capture ball above Vol. 02: hover over or tap Vol. 01 to find it. Play the battle, use the laser cutter, and enter **nick**. Victory returns to the signature spread and unlocks a signing desk to its left. A loss or escape leaves you at your original book location. Drag or click any of the six preset designs to add them, arrange and resize, then **Save**. Click a submitted image in **To review** to inspect it, then drag it onto either sheet. Resize with the corner handles and drag between pages directly. **Save** publishes the whole collection for everyone. Without the shared service, the desk offers a preview and cannot publish. Battle sounds can be muted. [Battle asset sources](dist/assets/battle/README.md) are included in the repository.
